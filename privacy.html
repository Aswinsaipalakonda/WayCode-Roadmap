Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide our development gateway services, including your authentication profile (via OAuth), repository metadata, and high-level intent prompts submitted for task execution.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly to authenticate your sessions, manage persistent Redis queue jobs, execute tasks inside isolated Docker containers, and dispatch notification updates regarding your build statuses.

3. Secret Management & Security

Sensitive API keys and personal credentials remain strictly server-side within secure environment stores and are never exposed to the client mobile browser or shared with third parties.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your repository workspace data, please reach out via our GitHub repository issues page.
